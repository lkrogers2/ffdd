Family Friendly

How Parenthood, Paid Leave, and Childcare Benefits Shape Educators’ Career Trajectories

Teaching is often described as a family-friendly profession, yet compared to similar fields it lacks systematic family-supportive policies. This study investigates how variation in family-friendly policies—including paid parental leave, childcare supports, and flexible work arrangements—affects educator career outcomes such as turnover, absenteeism, and promotion.

Research Projects
Part I

Family-Friendly District Database

A first-of-its-kind national database documenting the prevalence and features of family-friendly policies across a stratified sample of U.S. school districts, covering parental leave, childcare supports, group benefits, and flexible work.

Part II

North Carolina Longitudinal Case Study

A state-level longitudinal study linking administrative education data with vital records to examine how childbirth affects educators’ career trajectories and how family-friendly policies moderate these outcomes.
